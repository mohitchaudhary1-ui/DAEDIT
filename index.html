Questions

Good to know.

Do I need any experience or a design background?

No. Every course starts from the basics. You need curiosity and the time to practise between classes.

Do I need to know coding for the AI agents or websites courses?

No. Both courses use no-code tools. You build working agents and live websites without writing code.

Can I pay the fee in instalments?

Yes, EMI is available. Ask about the payment plans at your counselling session.

How does the ₹25,000 monthly scholarship work?

A daédit test is held on the last Sunday of every month. The top scorer wins the ₹25,000 scholarship. The test is open to enrolled students and new applicants.

Who gets the assured job?

Selected candidates who keep 85% attendance, pass every monthly test and clear the final portfolio review.

I run a business. Which option suits me?

Start with a weekend workshop at ₹1,999 — two days, four hours a day. For deeper skills, the AI Tools & AI Agents Lab or Social Media & Meta Ads Pro fit business owners well.

How big are the batches?

20 seats per batch, so a mentor can review every piece of your work.
